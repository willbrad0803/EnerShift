About EnerShift

EnerShift is a North West UK SaaS platform built to make energy flexibility simple for businesses.

Right now, our dashboard gives you accurate wind forecasts for your postcode and clear recommendations on when to shift energy-intensive loads (compressors, refrigeration, EV charging, pumps, etc.). Many users are already saving £3,000–£12,000 per year by acting on these recommendations manually.

In the coming months we’re adding automation so the system can trigger the shifts for you automatically. This is when we expect the annual value to rise significantly — to £14,000–£45,000+ per site through combined bill savings and flexibility market earnings (BiTraDER, DFS, Balancing Mechanism).

Why the North West?

We are starting here because of HyNet decarbonisation projects, live BiTraDER trials, and ENWL territory. This gives us real local insight and fast feedback from early pilot sites.

Our Mission

To make energy flexibility accessible and profitable for every business that can shift load — turning green energy abundance into real savings and revenue.
